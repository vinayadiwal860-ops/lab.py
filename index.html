Why does solar activity matter?

Solar flares and solar wind can disturb Earth’s magnetosphere, affecting satellites, GPS signals, radio communication, astronaut safety, and power infrastructure.

  • Satellites can experience charging and communication interruptions.
  • GPS and navigation systems become less precise during strong geomagnetic storms.
  • Radio communication may be disrupted, especially at high latitudes.
  • Astronauts in orbit may face increased radiation exposure.
  • Power grids can be stressed by induced currents during major events.
